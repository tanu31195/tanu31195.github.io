About CoachDesk

CoachDesk is a mobile app designed for gym owners, personal trainers, and fitness coaches to manage their members, track attendance, handle memberships, and stay on top of payments — all from their phone.

Features

👥

Member Management

Add, edit, and view all your gym members in one place.

✅

Attendance Tracking

Mark daily attendance with one tap. View session history per member.

📦

Memberships & Packages

Create packages with session limits or unlimited access.

💳

Payment Tracking

Log payments and track outstanding balances easily.

🗓️

Schedule Management

Set up class time slots and manage your weekly schedule.

☁️

Cloud Sync

Your data syncs securely across devices automatically.
